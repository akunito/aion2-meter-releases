# Items
What your characters hold: the bag and warehouse of the character you play, the account's shared warehouse, the equipped set, and where every item is across your characters.
## What you see
- Today (top card): the character's content tickets as the server last sent them — Odyle energy (base + bonus), Shugo keys, the sanctuary raids' weekly entries and final-boss clears, the Conquest dungeons, the held Abyss corridors and the seal-dungeon hour — with the time to the daily reset (16:00 Seoul) and the weekly one (Wednesday at the same moment: 09:00 CEST, 08:00 CET from October 25; the same schedule as the footer's Reset and Weekly pills). The full list arrives at login, single changes as they happen. Of the party dungeons it lists only the Conquest ones of the Expedition window, by stars, with the guaranteed chest as progress/cap ("chest 18/21": +2 per Conquest clear, refreshed when you leave the dungeon; "claimable" in green once it is full; "chest ?/14" until the character has logged in or changed zone with the meter open) and the shared cube rewards (x/10), plus transcendence; Exploration and keys nobody has named yet are left out (they are still saved in character.json). Grey names are inferred from the game's id numbering (hover for the key).
- Achievements (collapsed by default): the title bar shows tiers claimed / total (the game's "completed" counter), achievements fully done, the number with a reward waiting (green chip) and those in progress. Open it for every achievement whose next tier is reached but not claimed, with the tier, the counter against its goal and the rewards. Counters update live; a claim shows after the server confirms it. The same data goes to characters\<server>-<name>\achievements.json.
- Bag: the character's bag from the login list. "login list" (green) means it came whole at login; "partial" (amber) with a warning means slots are missing (a file written by an older meter): log in again and it fixes itself. Only the stack count is shown: bag and warehouse sizes grow with expansions and are not in the packets.
- Character warehouse: "last seen HH:mm" once the warehouse window was opened with the meter running; "partial" when only deposits were seen; "never opened" until then. The game sends the warehouse only when its window opens.
- Account warehouse (the game's "server warehouse"): shared by every character of the account on the server, so the tab merges what each character saw, slot by slot, newest first. The chip says who updated it last; the warning line says which character read the full list and when, and which slots other characters changed afterwards (blue dot on those tiles).
- Equipped: the worn set and cosmetics, collapsed by default (click the card's title bar to open or close any card).
- Tiles: the frame colour is the grade (common grey, rare green, legendary blue, unique gold, epic orange, special teal), the number bottom-right the stack count, "+N" top-left the enchant. Each card can switch between tiles and a list (slot, where else the item is, unit price, value). Hover a tile for the details: name in the other language, grade, bound, this stack, everywhere, where, market price and where it comes from.
- Show filters equipment, materials or consumables & misc; Sort orders by game order, grade, name or market value. Value is the lowest market listing × count (Craft → Load market file…), tradable items only.
## Across characters
- Type in the search box (English or Spanish, accents ignored): matching tiles light up, the rest dim, and the right panel lists every match with a chip per character and place, its source and the Craft hint: recipes that use it, what they need, what the account has and what is missing. "Open in Craft →" jumps to the recipe in the Craft tab.
- The search also takes a grade word in English or Spanish: "legendary stone", "piedra legendaria", "unique", "épico" (4 letters or more, plurals fine).
- Several items share a name and differ only by grade (in English "Artisan's Refining Stone" is both the rare and the legendary one). A coloured tag after the name says the grade: common grey, rare green, legendary blue, unique gold, epic orange, special teal. In the Craft hint and the achievement rewards the grade appears in brackets only when the name alone is ambiguous.
- With an empty box the panel lists items split over three or more places, worth gathering in one.
- The footer has one row per character of the server: Kina, Kina (Bound), Abyss Points and Enhance Stone (currencies are bag stacks, so they are left out of the grids), then its content tickets — Odyle energy (base+bonus), Shugo keys, Daeva Pass and event pass levels, the seal-dungeon hour left ("—" until the meter has seen that character log in) — the tradable value and when the meter last saw it; hover a name for its raid entries/boss clears and held Abyss corridors. The account warehouse has its own row. Below 820 px Kina (Bound) and Event drop out.
- "Names: English / Español" switches item names, and every other game name too: it is Settings → Names (also the switch in Build and Prices).
