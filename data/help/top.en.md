# Top DPS
Three rankings, one per scope button: My fights (the best fight this meter has seen per player and class, 30 s or longer), Server (your server's ranking) and Europe. The class tabs filter each one.
## What you see
- My fights: rank, player, where and when, damage and DPS. Click a row for skill shares, opener, repeated sequences and casts per minute; "Open" shows the saved fight, with that player's Profile, Skills and Daevanion tabs beside the fight's when the meter knows their official page. "Load profile" (in the opened row) opens the same fight on its Profile tab and fetches the page if it is missing or older than 12 h.
- Server / Europe: the official ranking read from the public character pages, one page per 400 ms, so the first load takes a few seconds (a "Loading data…" box shows while it reads). Clicking a row (or its "Load profile") opens the player card in place of the list: name, class, server, legion and title, the tiles CP / ITEM LEVEL / RANK / SEEN, and the tabs Profile (gear laid out like the game's character screen: armour left, accessories right, weapons, wings and pet in the middle; Arcana; attributes and divine stats, details on hover), Skills (actives, stigmas and passives with their levels; stigmas not acquired in grey) and Daevanion (open/total per board and the summed bonuses). Tabs without data are not shown. "Back" or Esc returns to the list.
## Notes
- Rankings come from aion2index.com and the official character API; nothing about you is sent, only reads.
- My fights only knows the fights this meter recorded on this PC.
