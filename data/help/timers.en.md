# Boss timers
Field boss respawn countdowns for your server. Times come from the game's own field boss list and from the kills the meter sees; nothing is guessed.
## What you see
- One card per boss: portrait, name, the map, and when it was killed (or found dead). The big number on the right is the countdown to the respawn; it turns amber inside your notice time. ALIVE (up since …) when the meter saw it alive, SHOULD BE UP when the countdown ran out, "—" when there is no time yet ("set respawn time", or "no timer in the game"). Hover the status to see where the time comes from: the in-game list (and when it was read) or the meter.
- The respawn chip (↻ 2 h ▾) is the time from a kill to the next spawn. "≈" means the meter measured it (the shortest time it saw from a kill to the boss showing up again); "from game" means the server's list gives the time.
- The chip next to the title is the server: every server runs its own bosses. It follows your character; pick another server there to see the timers your other characters collected.
- Bosses of quest and sealed-area instances have no world timer and are not listed.
## How to use it
- Open a map's field boss list in the game (map → Exploration → field monsters): its timers come straight from the server. Field bosses you see die restart their countdown on their own.
- Killed starts the countdown now (a kill the meter missed); the respawn chip sets the interval (15 min to 24 h); the bin removes the boss from the list.
- The bell watches a boss: "Notify before respawn" (off, 1, 2, 5 or 10 min) then plays a chime before it spawns. "🔔 Watched" shows the watched ones only. Right-click the boss pill in the meter's footer to switch the sound or the Windows notification (the notification is off by default: a toast over the game costs FPS).
- The boss pill in the footer shows the next field boss to spawn on your map (else on the server); clicking it opens this tab. Ctrl+Shift+T (editable in Settings) and the tray menu open the same list as a separate window; the pop-out icon opens another card on this tab.
